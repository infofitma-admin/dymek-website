Metrology, Inspection & Failure Analysis

Better yield depends on two things: catching variation early, and finding root causes fast when failures happen. Dymek provides metrology, inspection, manufacturing-data and failure-analysis solutions for wafers, thin films, packages and assembled electronic systems.

We support you from process development through production and reliability analysis.

Discuss your applicationSee capabilities

Thin-Film & Optical Process Metrology

Non-contact optical metrology measures film thickness, refractive index, extinction coefficient, reflectance, transmittance and multilayer structures. It works across dielectrics, resists, semiconductor layers and transparent films for mapping, uniformity control and production monitoring.

Small-spot reflectometry and non-contact 3D/OCT metrology extend this to patterned regions, rough surfaces, transparent films and subsurface interfaces. Examples include photoresist, MEMS, LED, VCSEL and advanced-packaging structures.

Interferometric surface metrology adds wafer flatness, thickness variation and roughness measurement close to the process, including areas where vibration isolation is limited.

Wafer-Level Automated Optical Inspection

Automated optical inspection (AOI) finds particles, scratches, pattern defects, process abnormalities and other visual anomalies. It works on substrates, bare wafers, patterned wafers and packaged devices.

AI-assisted defect detection and classification can reduce manual review, improve consistency and link inspection results to process optimization. Systems can be configured for novel materials, compound semiconductors, photonics and MEMS as well as conventional silicon.

X-Ray & CT Inspection

X-ray inspection gives non-destructive access to buried structures inside semiconductor packages and electronic assemblies. We support 2D X-ray, 3D CT and automated inline inspection for solder joints, micro-bumps, Cu pillars, TSV-related structures, die attach, package voids and other internal features.

High-resolution systems suit laboratory analysis, process development or production inspection.

Scanning Acoustic Microscopy

Acoustic microscopy is especially good at finding planar defects and interface problems inside bonded wafers and packaged devices. Typical findings include:

  • Delamination and voids
  • Non-bonded interfaces and die cracks
  • Underfill quality and molding defects
  • Thermal-interface layer issues

Because the method is non-destructive, you can use it for process characterization, reliability analysis and production screening.

Electrical, Magnetic & Device Characterization

This includes four-point-probe resistivity, wafer and chip probing, optoelectronic testing and magnetic thin-film measurement for MRAM and sensors. Probe systems range from ultra-low-signal I-V/C-V to high-current, high-voltage power devices, RF/sub-THz, magnetic-field-dependent and temperature-controlled testing.

Electron Microscopy & High-Resolution Analysis

Some defects need high-resolution structural or materials analysis. Scanning and transmission electron microscopy (SEM and TEM) provide detailed imaging and compositional information. They support process development, contamination analysis, package investigations, materials characterization and root-cause analysis.

Laser Sample Preparation for Failure Analysis

Targeted sample preparation is often the slowest step in failure analysis. We provide laser-based preparation systems that remove large volumes of material quickly and expose regions of interest in wafers, advanced packages and system-level assemblies.

Applications include:

  • Large-area package cross-sections
  • Flip-chip and solder structures
  • MEMS cap removal and deep-structure access
  • Targeted preparation of TSVs, RDLs, interposers, hybrid-bonded stacks and other buried interfaces

Fast bulk removal can cut FIB or PFIB time before precise end-pointing, final polishing and downstream SEM, TEM, X-ray or atom-probe analysis.

Manufacturing Data, Yield Analytics & Software

Manufacturing software brings together fabrication, metrology, inspection, assembly and test data. Use it for yield analytics, defect management, SPC, automated reporting, outlier detection, lot disposition, wafer and assembly mapping and equipment monitoring.

From Measurement Development to Production

We can take a measurement or inspection requirement from feasibility testing to a repeatable production workflow. We integrate instruments with handling, robotics, machine vision, fixtures and factory software. Results can feed databases, pass/fail logic and custom reporting or analysis scripts.
