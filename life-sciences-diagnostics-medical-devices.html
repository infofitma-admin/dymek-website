Industry · Life sciences

Life Sciences, Diagnostics & Medical Device Equipment

Life-science research and product manufacturing increasingly combine advanced microscopy, precision manufacturing, microfluidics, optical systems and automated quality control. Dymek supports researchers and manufacturers of diagnostic, genomic and medical-device products across imaging, microfabrication, inspection and materials characterization.

Advanced Microscopy & Biological Imaging

We provide scanning and transmission electron microscopy solutions for high-resolution imaging of biological samples. Applications range from surface morphology and cellular ultrastructure to tissue imaging, volume electron microscopy, tomography and structural biology.

Electron microscopy complements optical and fluorescence methods by revealing structures at nanometer resolution. It supports research in cell biology, microbiology, neuroscience, pathology, biomaterials and related fields.

2

Biomedical Optical Metrology & Assembly

Optical metrology supports biomedical and ophthalmic applications that need fast, non-contact measurement. Examples include contact lenses and lens molds, and research measurements of the corneal surface and tear-film thickness, where subject motion can limit conventional interferometry.

Precision active alignment also supports medical optical assemblies such as endoscopes and microscope objectives. In these, the centering and tilt of each lens element directly affect image quality and repeatability.

3

Genomics & Diagnostic Manufacturing

Diagnostic and sequencing consumables often contain dense arrays of small features that need consistent manufacturing quality. We provide automated optical inspection and defect-analysis technologies for precision substrates, patterned surfaces and other diagnostic components.

AI-assisted inspection helps manufacturers find particles, surface defects, process variation and other anomalies with less manual review.

Microfluidics, Biochips & Lab-on-Chip

Microfluidic and lab-on-chip devices put channels, chambers, sensors and functional surfaces into compact substrates. We support wafer bonding, nanoimprint, hot embossing, lithography, surface processing and other fabrication methods for microfluidic devices.

Applications include:

  • Blood and DNA analysis
  • Biosensors and cell sorting
  • Organ-on-chip platforms
  • Drug-delivery research

Non-contact surface and volumetric metrology measures microfluidic cells, transparent layers and internal interfaces without touching delicate structures. Surface topography, layer thickness and subsurface geometry can be checked during process development or quality control.

5

Precision Glass & Internal Channel Structures

Glass is widely used in diagnostics, microfluidics and optical-biological devices thanks to its chemical stability, optical properties and compatibility with microfabrication. We support precision laser cutting, micro-drilling and structuring of glass for diagnostic slides, biochips and specialized devices. This includes through-holes and fluidic access features, where conventional machining can cause chipping, cracking or excess stress.

For emerging applications, internal channel structures inside glass can also be explored for fluid handling, thermal control and other functions that are hard to machine conventionally.

6

Biomedical Thin Films & Nanomaterials

Thin-film metrology measures local coating thickness and optical properties on small or patterned biomedical parts. Applications include parylene coatings, polymer films and balloon walls or other thin transparent structures that need microscale measurement spots.

For research, vacuum-deposited nanoparticle layers support studies in drug delivery, antimicrobial and antiviral surfaces, biofilms, biosensors and other nanomedicine or diagnostic concepts. We support flexible R&D deposition systems for researchers developing these materials.

7

Biomaterials & Medical-Device Tribology

We support characterization of medical-device materials, coatings and interfaces through surface metrology, friction and wear testing, scratch and adhesion measurement and other mechanical tests.

Applications include implants, catheters, coatings, polymers and other parts where surface behavior and durability affect performance. For catheters and other minimally invasive devices, testing quantifies lubricity and insertion, push, navigation and removal forces, and links them to changes in surface condition. Custom fixtures, inspection and automation support scale-up where standard equipment does not fit.
