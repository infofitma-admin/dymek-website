Semiconductor Manufacturing & Advanced Packaging Equipment

Equipment, software and engineering support from the first wafer process to the finished package. We serve R&D labs, pilot lines and volume fabs working with silicon, compound semiconductors, power devices, MEMS and sensors.

Where are you in the process?

01

Wafer Processing & Device Fabrication

Lithography and resist processing, wafer bonding and layer transfer, plasma etch and deposition, thermal processing, cleaning, wafer separation and precision laser processing. Process-control metrology can be added around each step for silicon, MEMS, compound semiconductors, SiC/GaN power devices, sensors and other specialty wafers.

Explore Wafer Processing
02

Advanced Packaging & Assembly

Wafer-to-wafer and die-to-wafer bonding, hybrid bonding, temporary bonding and debonding, thin-wafer handling, precision die placement, flip-chip and thermocompression assembly, and 2.5D/3D integration. Typical applications are chiplets, HBM, fan-out, SiP, wafer-level and optical packaging.

Explore Advanced Packaging
03

Metrology, Inspection & Failure Analysis

Optical thin-film measurement, automated wafer inspection, X-ray/CT, scanning acoustic microscopy, electrical probing, electron microscopy and surface characterization. Failure-analysis workflows can also include fast laser sample preparation before SEM, FIB, TEM or other high-resolution analysis.

Explore Metrology
Across all paths

Manufacturing Data & Yield Analytics

Manufacturing software brings together data from fabrication, metrology, inspection, assembly and test. Use it for yield analytics, defect management, SPC, wafer and assembly mapping, automated reporting and equipment monitoring. Custom scripts and integrations can handle specialized engineering or reporting workflows.

05

Equipment Service & Fab Support

Repair, refurbishment, replacement, upgrades, field service, spare parts and relocation support for selected vacuum pumps, RF generators and matching networks, legacy thermal equipment and related subsystems.

Explore Equipment Service
FAQ

Common questions

Which semiconductor processes does Dymek support?

Dymek supports wafer processing (lithography, bonding, etch, deposition, thermal processing, dicing and laser processing), advanced packaging, metrology and inspection, failure analysis, manufacturing data software and fab equipment service.

Does Dymek support R&D as well as production?

Yes. Our technologies serve R&D laboratories, pilot lines and volume manufacturing.

Where does Dymek provide support?

Dymek has offices in Hong Kong, mainland China, Taiwan, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines.
