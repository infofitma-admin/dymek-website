Industry · Data storage

Data Storage & Magnetic Device Equipment

Dymek has long experience supporting data-storage manufacturing and magnetic thin-film applications. The market is moving from conventional hard-disk technology toward MRAM, advanced magnetic sensors and other spintronic devices, yet many core needs stay highly specialized: thin-film characterization, magnetic measurement and precision processing.

We provide measurement and process technologies for disk media, heads and sliders, magnetic multilayers, MRAM and GMR/TMR sensor devices.

Discuss your applicationSee capabilities
Track 01

Magnetic Thin-Film & Wafer Metrology

We provide non-contact magnetic measurement systems for magnetic multilayer wafers and thin films. Full-wafer mapping measures the magnetic properties used in development and production of data-storage, MRAM and magnetic-sensor devices.

Thin-film hysteresis and magnetoresistance measurement supports research and quality control of soft and hard magnetic materials on wafers and other samples.

Electrical probing under an applied magnetic field adds device-level characterization of magnetic sensors, MRAM structures and other magnetically responsive devices. It links film-level magnetic properties with electrical performance.

Track 02

Disk Media Thin-Film Metrology

Hard-disk media need precise control of ultra-thin protective and magnetic layers. We provide optical metrology that measures diamond-like carbon (DLC) thickness, optical properties and magnetic-layer thickness on disk media.

These measurements support process development, uniformity control and production quality assurance for advanced media.

Microscale optical film measurement adds local thickness and optical-property mapping on small, patterned or non-standard regions where large-spot measurement is not enough.

Track 03

Heads, Sliders & Precision Components

Data-storage heads and sliders need tight dimensional, surface and thin-film control. We support optical and surface metrology, thin-film characterization, microscopy and other measurement methods used during head and slider processing.

Where special handling or automation is needed, measurement equipment can be integrated with custom fixtures and motion systems.

Track 04

MRAM & Emerging Magnetic Memory

MRAM manufacturing needs control of very thin magnetic multilayers, annealing and magnetic orientation across the wafer. We support magnetic wafer mapping, electrical characterization, thin-film metrology and related measurement for MRAM development and production.

This extends our data-storage portfolio beyond disk media into semiconductor-based non-volatile memory.

Track 05

GMR/TMR Sensors & Magnetic Device Processing

Giant- and tunneling-magnetoresistance (GMR/TMR) devices are increasingly used in automotive, industrial and consumer sensors. We support selective laser annealing to set magnetic reference directions on integrated GMR/TMR sensors, together with magnetic and thin-film metrology for process control.

The same magnetic-device expertise now serves new markets beyond traditional storage.

Track 06

Surface, Tribology & Failure Analysis

Head-media interaction and precision mechanical parts may need analysis of roughness, friction, wear and coating durability. We provide surface metrology, tribology and high-resolution microscopy to investigate performance and failure mechanisms.

For research and production, these technologies can be combined with custom handling or automation for specialized samples.

Track 07

From Materials Development to Production

We combine magnetic and thin-film metrology, precision processing, surface characterization, failure analysis and custom handling around specialized storage or spintronic devices, from materials development through production quality control.
