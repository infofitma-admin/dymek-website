May 2023 · Penang, Malaysia

EV Group: local support for Malaysia’s fabs

EV Group (EVG) makes wafer bonding and lithography systems for MEMS, nanotechnology and semiconductors. Dymek has been a long-standing partner of EVG in several Asian countries.

Source: EV Group press release

Challenge

Malaysia is a major centre for semiconductor packaging, assembly and test, and chipmakers keep investing there. Customers using EVG systems needed faster, closer support for installation, service and spare parts.

Solution

EVG and Dymek formed a joint venture, EV Group Malaysia Dymek Sdn. Bhd., based in Penang. It works closely with EVG headquarters and handles:

  • Equipment installation
  • Technical service and support
  • Spare parts management and supply
  • Process development support
Result
  • A local EVG support team in Penang, planned to be fully running from July 2023
  • One partner for customers, from installation to process support
  • A stronger base for EVG customers across Southeast Asia
March 2025 · Southeast Asia

yieldWerx: faster answers on yield loss

yieldWerx makes analytics software that helps semiconductor companies manage yield and test data.

Source: EE Times Asia

Challenge

Fabless companies, OSATs, foundries and IDMs in Southeast Asia need to find and fix yield problems quickly. Slow answers mean downtime, higher costs and lower quality.

Solution

yieldWerx and Dymek signed a strategic partnership. yieldWerx brings its analytics platform; Dymek brings local knowledge and more than three decades of work with semiconductor manufacturers in Vietnam, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Malaysia and the Philippines.

Result
  • Region-specific yield management support for local manufacturers
  • Services aimed at smoother processes, better quality and lower operating costs
  • Local contacts who understand both the software and the fab
China & Southeast Asia

3D-Micromac: laser systems closer to Asian customers

3D-Micromac builds laser micromachining and roll-to-roll laser systems, including its microVEGA systems for the semiconductor industry.

Source: 3D-Micromac

Challenge

Asia is a key growth market for 3D-Micromac’s photovoltaic, medical device, electronics and semiconductor customers. The company wanted faster business development and closer customer support in the region.

Solution

3D-Micromac named Dymek as a new trade agency for China and Southeast Asia, adding Dymek’s regional knowledge and customer relationships to its sales and support network.

Result
  • Local contacts for 3D-Micromac laser systems in China and Southeast Asia
  • Support for fabs and device makers from first questions to after-sales service
  • A long-term partnership built on trust with local customers
