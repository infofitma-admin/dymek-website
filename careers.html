Strategic projects & operations

Strategic Projects & Operations Manager

Kuala Lumpur, MalaysiaFull-timePrimarily remote
Apply
View full job description

Reports to: Managing Director SEA · Travel: approx. 20–30% of working time, typically trips of 3–7 days, within Asia and further abroad · Working arrangement: primarily remote, with occasional in-person meetings

We are seeking a capable, commercially minded and personable Strategic Projects & Operations Manager to work directly with Dymek's senior management. You will represent Dymek at international exhibitions, customer meetings and, over time, visits to our equipment suppliers, while also taking on a broad range of business and operational projects across Asia and beyond.

A central part of this role is acting as an extension of senior management. You will begin by attending exhibitions and meetings alongside the Managing Director, learning Dymek's technologies, customers, suppliers and way of doing business. As your knowledge and confidence grow, you will increasingly represent Dymek on your own: presenting our message to customers, building relationships across the industry, and reporting back clearly on what you see and hear.

Alongside this, the role is hands-on. You will work across commercial, operational and corporate matters, some of which may initially be unfamiliar or loosely defined. Rather than waiting for assigned tasks, you will identify what needs to be done, organize the work, and move matters forward independently, taking on the detailed work yourself where needed.

We are looking for someone with sound business judgment, genuine curiosity about technology, and the confidence to represent a company professionally in front of customers and partners. Strong communication, conscientiousness, and the judgment to know when to act independently and when to involve management are central to success.

How the role will develop
  • Initially: Attend international exhibitions and conferences together with the Managing Director, learning Dymek's equipment suppliers, technologies, customers, and how Dymek presents itself.
  • Over time: Attend exhibitions independently or as part of the Dymek team, and represent Dymek at customer meetings, in person and virtually, alongside our local sales engineers.
  • Longer term: Visit Dymek's principals (equipment suppliers) on behalf of the company and take on a growing role in managing these relationships.
Representing Dymek: Exhibitions, Customers & Principals
  • Attend international exhibitions and conferences on behalf of Dymek, initially alongside the Managing Director and later independently or as part of the Dymek team.
  • Prepare for each exhibition by identifying the customers, principals and prospective suppliers to meet, and arranging meetings in advance.
  • At exhibitions, engage with existing and potential customers and principals, identify new technologies and potential new equipment suppliers, and gather market and competitor information.
  • After each event, prepare a concise report covering key contacts, leads, market insights and recommended next steps, and make sure leads are passed to the relevant sales engineers for follow-up.
  • Join customer meetings, in person and virtually, with Dymek's local sales engineers, presenting Dymek's company, capabilities and key messages as agreed with the Managing Director.
  • Report back promptly after customer meetings on what was discussed, customer requirements and concerns, opportunities identified, and agreed follow-up actions.
  • Over time, visit Dymek's principals to represent the company, discuss business performance and plans, and strengthen working relationships.
Project Execution & Problem-Solving
  • Take ownership of business and operational projects, from initial investigation and planning through to implementation and completion.
  • Work with senior management to identify, organize and prioritize matters requiring attention, including issues that do not fall neatly within an existing business function.
  • Investigate unfamiliar or ambiguous problems, gather relevant information, identify possible solutions, and recommend practical courses of action.
  • Coordinate colleagues, external parties and other resources as necessary, anticipating obstacles and following through on outstanding matters to keep projects moving.
  • Prepare clear, concise updates and recommendations, distinguishing matters you can resolve yourself from those requiring management decisions.
Commercial & Business Operations
  • Support the evaluation and execution of commercial opportunities, supplier relationships, and business arrangements.
  • Analyze commercial transactions and proposals, including supplier quotations, customer pricing, purchase orders, costs, gross margins, payment terms, and other relevant considerations.
  • Support business planning, budgeting, financial analysis, and management reporting, including gathering and interpreting information from Dymek's various offices.
  • Conduct commercial and market research to support the evaluation of new equipment suppliers, business partnerships, technologies, and geographic opportunities.
  • Review business processes and identify opportunities to improve coordination, efficiency, documentation, and operational effectiveness.
Corporate & Organizational Projects
  • Support the establishment and development of overseas offices, subsidiaries, joint ventures, and other business operations.
  • Coordinate with accountants, legal advisers, banks, corporate secretaries, government authorities, and other professional service providers, and assist with commercial agreements, corporate documentation, and compliance matters, working with specialists where necessary.
  • Support recruitment, compensation reviews, employment arrangements, organizational changes, and other HR-related projects in coordination with local management and advisers.
  • Assist with special projects and management initiatives as business needs evolve, including matters outside the usual scope of day-to-day operations.
International Coordination & Communication
  • Work closely with Dymek's colleagues across different countries, helping to coordinate activities, share information, and follow through on agreed actions.
  • Build effective working relationships with business partners, professional advisers, and other external parties, communicating clearly and diplomatically with people from different countries, cultures, and professional backgrounds.
  • Travel regularly within Asia and further abroad for exhibitions, customer meetings, principal visits, and ongoing projects.
Working Methods & Continuous Improvement
  • Make effective use of digital tools, including AI assistants, to speed up research, analysis, and documentation, while checking accuracy, recognizing limitations, and maintaining appropriate confidentiality.
  • Maintain organized project records and follow-up processes, and introduce practical improvements that reduce unnecessary manual work.
Qualifications & attributes
  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Science, Business Administration, Finance, Economics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Typically 6+ years of experience in business development, technical sales, business operations, commercial management, project management, or related roles.
  • Practical experience in a commercial B2B environment, preferably involving distribution, trading, manufacturing, engineering, or technical products and services. Exposure to semiconductor, optical, metrology, or scientific equipment is an advantage.
  • Confident presenter, comfortable presenting a company and its offering to customers, engineers, and senior executives, both in person and online.
  • Technical aptitude, with the ability to quickly understand technical products and applications and explain them clearly to others.
  • Professional presence and strong networking skills, with the ability to build rapport quickly with new contacts at exhibitions, meetings, and industry events.
  • Sound understanding of commercial transactions, including quotations, purchase orders, supplier costs, customer pricing, margins, and payment arrangements.
  • Strong analytical ability and financial literacy, with the capacity to interpret business information and identify important commercial considerations.
  • Demonstrated initiative and resourcefulness, with a track record of independently investigating problems and seeing work through to completion.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, including the ability to write clear, concise reports on meetings and events.
  • Conversational proficiency in Mandarin and/or Cantonese is strongly preferred, given Dymek's business activities across Greater China and the wider Asia region. Full professional fluency is not required.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and cultural awareness, with the ability to work effectively with colleagues, customers, principals, and external advisers across different countries.
  • Conscientious and dependable, with strong attention to detail, personal accountability, and sound judgment, including knowing when management involvement or specialist advice is required.
  • Proficient with Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude productively in a professional environment would be an advantage.
  • Willing and able to travel internationally on a regular basis, including multi-day exhibitions and trips beyond Southeast Asia.
What we offer
  • Direct exposure to senior management and a broad view of an established international business.
  • The opportunity to attend leading international exhibitions and meet world-class technology suppliers and customers across the semiconductor, optics, and advanced manufacturing industries.
  • Benefits including annual leave, medical coverage, EPF/SOCSO contributions, and AWS.
  • Flexible, primarily remote working arrangement.
  • Room to grow the role and take on greater responsibility as Dymek's business expands.

Application & Field Service Engineering

Service & Applications Engineer

Pathum Thani, ThailandFull-time
Apply
View full job description

We are seeking a technically minded and dependable Service & Applications Engineer to join our Thailand regional office in Pathum Thani. In this role, you will install, maintain and support the advanced manufacturing and metrology equipment we supply to semiconductor, optics, electronics and data storage manufacturers across Thailand, while working closely with our sales team to help customers choose and get the most out of the right tools. It is well suited to an engineer early in their career who is eager to learn; you will receive structured product training from our principals in Europe, the US and Asia, along with on-the-job guidance from senior engineers across our regional offices.

Field service & technical support
  • Installing, commissioning and qualifying new equipment at customer sites in line with supplier procedures and customer acceptance criteria
  • Performing scheduled preventive maintenance, calibration and system upgrades to keep installed tools running to specification
  • Diagnosing and resolving hardware, software and process issues on site or remotely, escalating to suppliers with clear technical reports when needed
  • Responding promptly to service calls and equipment breakdowns to minimise customer downtime
  • Managing spare parts requirements, including identifying parts, raising requests and tracking orders through to delivery
  • Preparing service reports, installation records and field documentation after every visit
  • Following customer cleanroom, safety and site access requirements at all times
Applications & sales support
  • Supporting the sales team with technical input during customer meetings, product presentations and equipment evaluations
  • Preparing and running equipment demonstrations, sample measurements and application trials for prospective customers
  • Understanding customer process requirements and recommending suitable equipment configurations and options
  • Providing technical input for quotations, specifications and tender documents
  • Delivering operator and basic maintenance training to customer engineers and technicians
  • Identifying opportunities for upgrades, service contracts and additional equipment within the installed base
Supplier coordination & technical development
  • Attending product and service training provided by our principals, locally or overseas, and building expertise across our product portfolio
  • Acting as the local technical point of contact between customers and supplier engineers, including coordinating visits by overseas specialists
  • Keeping suppliers informed on installed base status, recurring issues and customer feedback
  • Sharing knowledge with engineers in other Dymek offices and contributing to internal technical documentation
Operations & administration
  • Maintaining accurate records of the installed base, service history, warranties and service contracts
  • Coordinating with the office team on the import of tools and parts, customs documentation and delivery schedules
  • Looking after demo equipment, tools and test kits, ensuring they are calibrated and ready for use
  • Submitting service reports, expense claims and travel plans on time
Qualifications
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Electrical, Electronic, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Physics, Materials Engineering or a related field
  • Experience: fresh graduates are welcome to apply; 1 to 3 years of experience in field service, equipment maintenance or applications within the semiconductor, optics, electronics or data storage industry is a plus
  • Technical knowledge: solid grounding in electrical and mechanical systems, with the ability to read schematics, drawings and technical manuals. Exposure to metrology, optical inspection, vacuum or wafer processing equipment is an advantage
  • Technical skills: comfortable with Microsoft Office and basic PC and network troubleshooting; familiarity with equipment control software, data analysis or scripting is a bonus
  • Key attributes: a methodical and safety-conscious approach to troubleshooting; the ability to stay calm and organised under production pressure; and a proactive, adaptable and teachable attitude
  • Communication: fluent in Thai with good written and spoken English. Able to explain technical issues clearly to both engineers and non-technical customers
  • Mobility: willing to travel frequently to customer sites across Thailand, particularly the industrial estates around Bangkok, Pathum Thani, Ayutthaya and the Eastern Seaboard, with occasional overseas travel for training. A valid Thai driving licence is preferred

Application & Field Service Engineering

Service Manager

SingaporeFull-time
Apply
View full job description

We are seeking a capable, hands-on Service Manager to lead the installation, servicing and technical support of the metrology and process equipment Dymek supplies to customers in the semiconductor, optics, biotech, aerospace and R&D sectors. Based in Singapore, you will hold primary responsibility for service delivery in the local market while contributing your expertise to projects across the wider Asia region. This is very much a hands-on role: while you will coordinate and mentor a small team of field service engineers, you are expected to remain on the tools yourself. You will also be the face of Dymek and its principals in front of customers, communicating clearly how an issue has been resolved and building lasting confidence among customers and suppliers.

Field service & technical delivery
  • Install, commission, service and repair metrology and process equipment at customer sites, working hands-on alongside the field service team
  • Diagnose and troubleshoot faults across a broad range of equipment, including tools you may not have encountered before, by reasoning from first principles, technical manuals and supplier documentation
  • Provide on-site and remote technical support to customers, ensuring equipment is restored to full working condition with minimal disruption
  • Contribute hands-on expertise to installation and service projects across the Asia region as required, involving regular regional travel
Team coordination & mentoring
  • Coordinate and schedule the field service team’s workload, allocating jobs based on skills, location and priority
  • Mentor and develop junior engineers, sharing diagnostic methods and building the team’s technical capability over time
  • Serve as the senior point of escalation for complex or sensitive technical issues
Customer & supplier communication
  • Act as the primary technical liaison with customers, communicating clearly how issues have been diagnosed and resolved
  • De-escalate difficult situations and manage customer expectations with professionalism and composure
  • Build and sustain trust with customers and suppliers that Dymek consistently delivers reliable engineering solutions
  • Liaise with equipment suppliers (principals) on technical matters, warranty claims and escalations
Commercial contribution
  • Identify and follow up on opportunities for service contract renewals
  • Spot and flag opportunities for spare parts sales, upgrades and additional services that benefit the customer
  • Support the sales team with technical input during the pre-sales and quotation process where relevant
Documentation & spare parts
  • Prepare clear, accurate service reports, installation records and maintenance documentation for every job
  • Maintain organised records of service history, customer communications and technical findings
  • Manage and track spare parts inventory, ensuring critical parts are available to support timely service
Qualifications
  • Diploma or degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Mechatronics, Electronics Engineering or a related technical field
  • Proven field service experience with precision instruments, capital equipment or technical systems; a background in metrology, semiconductor, optics or related industries is an advantage but not essential
  • Strong, demonstrable ability to troubleshoot unfamiliar equipment independently by interpreting technical manuals, schematics and engineering documentation
  • Comfortable taking ownership of technical issues and working independently, while knowing when to seek support from colleagues or equipment suppliers
  • Some experience mentoring, guiding or coordinating junior engineers or technical staff would be an advantage
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain technical issues clearly, handle challenging situations professionally and inspire customer and supplier confidence
  • Conscientious and disciplined about documentation
  • Willingness to travel regularly within Singapore and across the Asia region as projects require

Operations & Administration

Accounts & Office Operations Administrator

MalaysiaFull-time
Apply
View full job description

We are seeking a meticulous and versatile Accounts & Office Operations Administrator to help keep our office running smoothly day to day. In this role, you will handle the full accounting cycle for the organisation while assisting across sales administration, logistics, payroll and general office functions. It is well suited to someone early in their career who is eager to learn; you will develop full-set accounting and supporting administrative functions on the job, with guidance from the company secretary, auditors and the wider team. We value reliability, discretion and a proactive, teachable mindset over rigid role boundaries.

Accounting & finance
  • Progressively taking on the full accounting cycle, with guidance and training
  • Recording and verifying supplier invoices, matching them against Purchase Orders (PO) and Delivery Orders (DO)
  • Processing supplier payments (AP) and issuing customer invoices while monitoring the receivables ledger for timely collections (AR)
  • Handling monthly bank reconciliations, performing journal entries and managing petty cash
  • Preparing the Profit & Loss Account, Balance Sheet and Trial Balance
  • Managing monthly staff salary processing alongside statutory deductions and submissions (EPF, SOCSO and PCB/LHDN)
  • Maintaining proper documentation for all financial records
  • Liaising directly with banks, the company secretary, auditors and tax agents
Sales administration
  • Providing backend support for sales personnel to ensure seamless commercial operations
  • Preparing and formatting customer quotations
  • Processing sales orders and invoices
  • Registering and maintaining Dymek’s status as an approved vendor in customer procurement systems
  • Managing end-to-end logistics and shipment administration, coordinating inbound and outbound shipments and tracking delivery status
  • Preparing shipping documentation such as commercial invoices, packing lists, delivery notes and airway bills
  • Maintaining proactive communication with suppliers, customers and freight forwarders to confirm on-time deliveries
  • Compiling operational reports and handling shipment claims for damaged or delayed goods
Office administration
  • Overseeing general office upkeep, including office utilities, supplies and cleaning services
  • Maintaining organised physical and digital filing systems
  • Handling banking or corporate errands
  • Assisting colleagues and field engineers with expense claims, travel requirements and compensation
  • Supporting basic HR activities, including organising office events and team-building initiatives
Qualifications
  • Education: Diploma or degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration or a related field
  • Experience: fresh graduates are highly welcome to apply; up to 2 years of relevant experience is a plus but not required
  • Core knowledge: basic understanding of accounting principles, with a genuine willingness to be trained on full-set accounts, payroll and logistics. Awareness of Malaysian statutory requirements (EPF, SOCSO, PCB/LHDN) is an advantage
  • Technical skills: comfortable with Microsoft Office, particularly Excel; exposure to accounting software is a bonus
  • Key attributes: a sharp eye for precision; strong organisational skills to handle varied tasks simultaneously; and a proactive, adaptable and teachable attitude
  • Communication: good communication skills and the strict discretion required to handle confidential financial and staff information
