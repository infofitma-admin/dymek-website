Semiconductor Equipment Service & Fab Support

Fabs depend on complex equipment and subsystems that must keep running long after installation. Dymek supports selected process tools and fab subsystems through repair, refurbishment, replacement, upgrades and field service.

Our goal is simple: help you extend equipment life, reduce unplanned downtime and keep access to critical support for current and legacy systems.

01

Vacuum Pump Repair, Refurbishment & Replacement

Vacuum pumps are critical to many deposition, etch, thermal and analytical processes. We support selected vacuum pump models through repair, refurbishment, exchange or replacement programs, depending on your installed equipment and needs.

Support can include evaluating failed units, coordinating overhaul work, sourcing replacements and handling logistics to your regional sites.

02

RF Generator & Matching Network Support

RF generators and matching networks are essential subsystems in plasma-based equipment. We support repair, refurbishment and replacement of selected RF power and matching equipment used in etch, deposition and related processes.

This gives you another path for aging or hard-to-source subsystems, so you don't have to replace an otherwise serviceable process tool.

03

Legacy Furnace & Thermal Equipment Support

Installed diffusion, oxidation and LPCVD furnaces can stay useful for many years when controls, gas systems and key mechanical parts are maintained. We support selected legacy thermal equipment with upgrades, refurbishment, spare parts and technical service.

Where it makes sense, older systems can be modernized to improve controls, reliability or compatibility with current process requirements.

04

Equipment Relocation, Installation & Field Service

We coordinate de-installation, relocation, re-installation, commissioning and field service for selected equipment. Our regional team supports customers running several manufacturing sites across Asia. Where needed, we coordinate with equipment manufacturers and specialist service partners.

05

Spare Parts & Obsolescence Support

Obsolete components can cause outsized downtime on mature equipment. We help source replacement parts, identify compatible alternatives and coordinate repair or refurbishment of selected subsystems.

For equipment nearing end of support, we help you decide which path is most practical: refurbishment, subsystem replacement or full tool replacement.

06

Flexible Support for Mature & Specialty Fabs

Not every fab runs only the newest production equipment. Research fabs, MEMS lines, power semiconductor facilities and specialty manufacturers often rely on mature tools that are still technically capable but need flexible support. We work with these customers to keep useful equipment running and to find practical upgrade or replacement options.
