Industrial & Precision Manufacturing Solutions

Industrial and precision manufacturers often face inspection and process challenges that don't fit one industry. Dymek provides metrology, materials testing, laser processing, machine vision, software and automation for precision machining, foundry, marine, energy, heavy industry and other advanced manufacturing.

DWG-01Scale 1:1

Automated 3D Inspection & Dimensional Metrology

We provide non-contact 3D inspection for automated dimensional measurement and surface checks on the production floor. Systems combine precision optics, machine vision, robotics and computational analysis to measure complex parts without targets or manual probing.

Applications include castings, machined components, marine propellers, composite structures, large fabricated parts and other components that are hard to inspect automatically with conventional CMM methods.

Portable and robot-mounted surface gauges complement full-field inspection. They measure roughness, scratches, pits, edge breaks, radii, corrosion and other local features directly on complex parts. This helps when sending a large part to a metrology lab is impractical.

DWG-02Rev A

Machine Vision & Automated Defect Inspection

Automated optical inspection finds scratches, contamination, surface defects, missing features and other visual problems, with less manual inspection. Machine-vision systems are configured around your part geometry, production rate and defect criteria.

Inspection can be integrated with robotics, conveyors, part handling and production software for inline or near-line quality control.

DWG-03Rev A

Materials, Coatings & Tribology

We provide lab systems for testing friction, wear, hardness, adhesion, roughness, coatings, lubricants and other material or surface properties. Controlled temperature, humidity, vacuum and load reproduce demanding operating environments.

These tools support R&D, supplier qualification, product development and quality control for bearings, gears, coatings, polymers, metals and other industrial materials.

DWG-04Rev A

Precision Laser Processing

Laser micromachining cuts, structures, ablates or modifies metals, polymers, glass, ceramics and other materials with minimal mechanical force. Precision micro-drilling suits nozzles, filters, sieves, fluid-handling parts and other components that need very small holes, high aspect ratios or controlled hole geometry.

We support standard laser-processing equipment and custom process development for unusual geometries or materials, including production processes with automated handling.

DWG-05Rev A

Large & Complex Components

Large industrial parts bring unique metrology and handling challenges. We support inspection for wind-energy components, marine propellers, heavy castings and other large structures where portability, robot reach or full-field measurement matters.

Custom fixtures and motion systems can be built when a part can't be positioned with standard lab equipment.

DWG-06Rev A

Data Analytics, Optimization & Custom Software

Manufacturing improvement depends more and more on how well production data is used. We support optimization, scheduling, equipment-utilization analysis, process monitoring, predictive models, dashboards and automated reporting alongside equipment and automation projects.

Custom software processes instrument data, generates reports, connects results to production databases and automates repetitive engineering work.

DWG-07Rev A

From Feasibility to Production

We combine robots, precision motion, machine vision, metrology, tooling and control software into semi- or fully automated systems. This includes mechanical design, safety, part handling, recipes, data collection and factory integration.

Projects move from feasibility testing through design, fabrication, installation and production support.
