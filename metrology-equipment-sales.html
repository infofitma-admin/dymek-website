What we supply

Our portfolio covers the measurement and inspection needs of advanced manufacturing:

  • Interferometric and dynamic optical metrology
  • Thin-film thickness and optical-property measurement
  • Non-contact 3D surface and topography metrology
  • X-ray/CT, scanning acoustic microscopy and automated optical inspection
  • Electron microscopy and laser sample preparation
  • Electrical, optoelectronic and magnetic probing
  • Tribology and materials characterization

Process equipment for bonding, lithography, nanoimprint, deposition, thermal and laser processing is also available. Browse by industry to see what fits your application.

How we help you buy with confidence

  • Application review. We start with your part, process and specification, not a catalogue.
  • Feasibility trials. Where possible, we arrange sample measurements or demos so you can see results on your own parts.
  • Clear proposals. We explain options and trade-offs in plain language, so your engineering and purchasing teams can decide together.
  • Import and logistics. Our regional offices handle the local steps of bringing equipment to your site.
  • Installation and training. We commission the system and train your operators and engineers.
  • Long-term support. Service, spare parts and upgrades stay close to you through our regional network.

Why buy through Dymek

Since 1989, we have worked as the link between global technology providers and industrial customers across Asia. You get local language support, local time zones and engineers who understand both the technology and your production reality.
