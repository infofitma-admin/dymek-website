How it works

From your sample to clear answers in four steps

  1. Tell us about it

    Share your part, material and what you need to measure or make.

  2. Send your sample

    We confirm the details and tell you where to ship, or bring it to one of our offices.

  3. We test it

    Our engineers and our principals’ application labs run the measurement or process trial.

  4. See the results

    You receive a clear report and can join a live or online demo to discuss it.

What we can test

Typical sample tests

Every test depends on the sample and the available tool. Tell us what you need and we will confirm what is possible.

Thin films & coatings

Thickness, refractive index and uniformity of films on wafers, glass and optics.

Surface shape & roughness

Flatness, form, step height and roughness of optics, wafers and machined parts.

Hidden defects

Voids, cracks and delamination inside packages, bonded wafers and assemblies.

Failure analysis

Sample preparation, cross-sections and microscopy to find the root cause.

Laser & process trials

Laser micromachining, bonding, deposition and other process tests on your parts.

Data & yield

Trial analysis of your test and inspection data to find yield loss.
