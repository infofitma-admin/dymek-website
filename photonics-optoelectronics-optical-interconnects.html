Photonics, Optoelectronics & Optical Interconnects

This sector covers photonic devices, integrated optical systems and high-speed optical communication hardware. Dymek provides process, integration, alignment and test technologies for lasers, VCSELs, LEDs, photodetectors, optical sensors, waveguides, PICs, optical I/O and co-packaged optics (CPO).

01

Photonic Device Fabrication

Photonic devices are made from many materials, including silicon-based materials, compound semiconductors and optical crystals. We provide plasma etching, deposition, wafer cleaning, surface treatment and precision laser processing for photonic device fabrication.

Processes support lasers, waveguides, detectors, microLEDs, gratings and other optical or optoelectronic structures. Laser lift-off also supports microLED and other compound semiconductor devices where functional layers must be separated from growth or carrier substrates.

PVD thin-film processes support photonic integrated circuits, optical interference stacks and specialized device materials. Flexible research platforms can also deposit superconducting or functional films for emerging quantum-photonic and sensor structures.

02

Wafer-Level Integration & Photonic Packaging

Modern photonic systems combine optical, electronic and mechanical structures in tightly integrated packages. We support wafer bonding, thin-wafer processing, lithography, nanoimprint and precision assembly for photonic integration.

Applications include optical coupling structures, grating couplers, beam-shaping elements, heterogeneous photonic integration and other architectures that combine several materials and device functions.

Precision assembly supports commercial laser, infrared, LiDAR, MEMS/CIS and optical-communication modules, using high-accuracy die placement and suitable bonding methods. For multi-element optics, active alignment centers lenses, collimators, apertures, reticles and detector planes across visible and infrared wavelengths.

03

Co-Packaged Optics & Optical I/O

AI infrastructure and high-performance computing are pulling optical interconnects closer to electronic processors. We support manufacturing processes for co-packaged optics and optical I/O, including wafer and die integration, optical coupling structures, precision alignment and photonic packaging.

Alignment technologies support lenses, fiber arrays and other optical interfaces that need precise positioning between photonic devices and external optical connections.

04

Photonic Process Metrology

Photonic manufacturing often needs dimensional and layer measurements in addition to optical testing. We support non-contact 3D topography, transparent-film thickness and multilayer or subsurface metrology for photonic wafers and device structures.

Applications include patterned photoresist on silicon or compound semiconductor wafers, post-development and pre-etch checks, transparent functional layers and microfabricated features in VCSELs, LEDs, MEMS and other photonic devices.

05

Optoelectronic Device Test

Optoelectronic devices need both electrical and optical characterization. We provide wafer- and chip-level probe systems for combined measurement of VCSELs, laser diodes, LEDs, photodiodes and avalanche photodiodes.

Depending on the device, measurements include:

  • Electrical characteristics
  • Emitted optical power and wavelength
  • Near- and far-field beam behavior
  • Frequency response
06

Manufacturing Data & Yield Analytics

Manufacturing software brings photonics data together across wafer fabrication, optical/electrical wafer test, assembly, burn-in and final test. Yield analytics, automated reporting and custom integrations link wafer-level measurements to assembly conditions and final device performance.

07

Precision Optical Alignment

Photonic modules often need micron or sub-micron alignment of lenses, fibers and optical axes. Passive or active alignment, precision motion, component handling and custom fixtures can be integrated with bonding, dispensing, curing and inspection.

08

Quantum Photonics & Emerging Integration

Emerging photonic and quantum devices can require low-temperature heterogeneous integration of materials such as silicon, lithium niobate and III-V semiconductors. These are hard to combine through conventional growth alone.

09

Fiber Optics & Specialized Photonic Manufacturing

Specialized photonic manufacturing can also include optical-fiber preform fabrication and related optical-communication processes.

10

From Photonic R&D to Production

For emerging photonic applications where standard equipment is not the full answer, we combine process equipment, alignment, metrology, fixtures, handling and automation around the workflow you need.
