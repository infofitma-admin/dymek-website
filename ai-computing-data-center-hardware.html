Industry · AI & HPC

AI Computing & Data Center Hardware Manufacturing

AI and high-performance computing are rapidly changing semiconductor architecture, memory bandwidth, optical interconnects and thermal management. Dymek provides process, packaging, inspection and photonics technologies for the hardware supply chain behind AI accelerators and next-generation data centers.

Our focus is on making and integrating the chips, packages, optical interfaces and advanced components inside high-performance computing systems. We do not supply facility-level data center infrastructure.

U01

AI Accelerators, HBM & Advanced Packaging

AI processors increasingly rely on high-bandwidth memory, chiplets, advanced substrates and 2.5D/3D integration. We support wafer-to-wafer and die-to-wafer bonding, hybrid bonding, thin-wafer handling, precision die placement and related heterogeneous-integration processes.

These technologies are used in HBM stacks, logic-memory integration, chiplet architectures, fan-out packaging and other high-density packages. They increase bandwidth while shortening interconnects and lowering power use.

U02

Co-Packaged Optics & High-Speed Optical Interconnects

Electrical interconnects are becoming a bottleneck, so optical links are moving closer to switches, processors and accelerators. We support silicon photonics, optical I/O and co-packaged optics through wafer bonding, nanoimprint, photonic device processing, precision optical alignment and optical/electrical device testing.

Applications include coupling structures, gratings, lenses, fiber-array alignment, photonic dies and other elements that connect high-speed optical networks directly to advanced electronic packages.

U03

Inspection, Yield & Failure Analysis

AI hardware combines expensive dies, dense interconnects and multiple stacked layers. Early defect detection and fast failure analysis matter more than ever. We provide automated optical inspection, X-ray and CT, scanning acoustic microscopy, thin-film metrology, electron microscopy and other methods for wafers and advanced packages.

Laser-based sample preparation speeds up access to regions of interest in complex packages before SEM, FIB or TEM analysis. Emerging workflows target TSVs, redistribution layers, interposers and buried bonding interfaces in 2.5D/3D packages.

U04

Manufacturing Data & Yield Analytics

AI accelerators, HBM and photonic modules combine costly dies and process steps, so yield learning and traceability are increasingly important. We provide software for process and yield analytics, defect management, statistical process control, wafer and assembly mapping, automated reporting and data correlation across fabrication, packaging and test.

Custom data integrations connect metrology, inspection and test results with your existing manufacturing systems. Engineers can spot process drift, isolate yield loss and find root causes faster.

U05

Optoelectronic Device Characterization

Optical interconnects rely on lasers, VCSELs, photodiodes and other optoelectronic devices that need electrical and optical testing. We support wafer- and chip-level probing, optical power and wavelength measurement, beam characterization and related evaluation during development and production.

U06

Thermal Management & Emerging Cooling Technologies

Rising power density creates new needs for heat spreading, cooling and fluid delivery close to high-performance devices. We support precision micromachining and custom process development for advanced thermal-management structures.

Emerging approaches include internal channels, microfluidic structures and other features in glass or related materials. For new applications, we help evaluate feasibility and develop the fixtures, handling and automation.

U07

From Feasibility to Production

AI hardware architectures change quickly and often need non-standard process combinations. We support feasibility work and integration of process tools, metrology, alignment, fixtures and automation as new packaging, photonic and thermal concepts move toward repeatable production.
