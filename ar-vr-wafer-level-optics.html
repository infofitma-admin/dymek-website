Optics · Micro & nano optics

AR/VR & Wafer-Level Optics Manufacturing

This sector covers wafer-scale and replicated manufacture of micro- and nanostructured optical components. Dymek supports AR/VR waveguides, diffractive optical elements, microlens arrays and compact wafer-level optical systems, from early process development to scalable production.

Discuss your applicationSee capabilities
01/ 06

Micro- & Nano-Optical Structures

Nanoimprint lithography is a scalable way to replicate micro- and nanoscale optical structures across wafer-size substrates. We support processes for diffractive optical elements, optical waveguides, gratings, microlenses and other structured optical surfaces.

Technologies include master fabrication, UV nanoimprint, hot embossing and transfer of optical structures into production substrates. Nanoimprint also scales to large-area panel substrates for displays and photonic structures such as wire-grid polarizers. Applications go beyond AR/VR into sensing, illumination, imaging and other miniaturized optical systems.

02/ 06

Wafer-Level Lens Manufacturing & Assembly

Wafer-level manufacturing makes and assembles large numbers of optical elements in parallel. Capabilities include microlens molding, wafer-level replication, precision alignment, UV bonding and aligned stacking of lens and spacer wafers.

These processes support wafer-level cameras, 3D sensing, infrared sensing, compact imaging modules and other systems where size, alignment and throughput are critical.

High-precision die attach and optoelectronic assembly also support AR/VR emitters, image sensors, MEMS devices and other compact optical modules that need micron-level placement and reliable bonding.

03/ 06

AR/VR Waveguide & High-Index Glass Processing

AR/VR eyepieces and waveguides often use high-index glasses and other transparent crystalline materials that are hard to process mechanically. Precision laser technologies can separate and process these materials at wafer and component level.

Applications include singulation of AR waveguides and eyepieces, laser cutting, structuring and other processing of transparent substrates with minimal chipping and mechanical damage.

04/ 06

Etch, Deposition & Photonic Processing

Micro- and nano-optical devices may need semiconductor-style fabrication alongside conventional optical manufacturing. We provide plasma etching, thin-film deposition, surface treatment and related equipment for optical and photonic materials.

These support waveguides, gratings, microLED structures and other devices that combine optical function with microfabricated features.

Thin-film coating processes also support:

  • Precision waveguide coatings
  • Hard anti-reflective surfaces
  • Transparent conductive layers and electrochromic structures
  • Controlled transmission or color on smart-glass and wearable-display parts

For microLED and related display technologies, laser processes also support high-throughput lift-off and transfer, plus local repair or rework of defective dies and display elements during production.

05/ 06

Metrology & Process Control

Measurement is needed throughout wafer-level optical manufacturing to control substrates, films and finished optical performance. We support thin-film thickness and optical-property measurement, surface and topography characterization, and interferometric measurement of optical components and systems.

High-speed non-contact 3D metrology inspects microlens arrays, diffractive features and transparent films. Display-oriented measurements quantify photo-spacer geometry, color-filter structures, local defects and other features that cause optical non-uniformity.

Automated inspection and machine vision can also be adapted to detect defects and process variation across wafers and optical components.

06/ 06

From Process Development to Production

New optical designs may combine imprinting, laser processing, bonding, deposition and metrology with no standard equipment setup. We support feasibility trials, custom tooling, handling and inspection, then automate the process as it moves to pilot or volume manufacturing.
