Optics · Components

Precision Optics & Optical Manufacturing Equipment

Precision optical components need tight control of geometry, surface quality, coatings and alignment. Dymek provides equipment to make, characterize and assemble lenses, mirrors, windows, prisms, filters and other optical components, from process development to final inspection and assembly.

01

Optical Metrology

We provide interferometric measurement systems for precision optical components and complete optical systems. They measure surface form, radius of curvature, transmitted wavefront and other parameters that verify optical performance.

Systems are available for flat, spherical, aspheric and other challenging surfaces, at visible and infrared wavelengths. Vibration-insensitive technology allows accurate measurement directly in manufacturing areas, so parts don't need to travel to an isolated metrology lab.

Non-contact 3D surface metrology adds production measurement for aspheric and freeform optics, diamond-turned parts and molds, microlens arrays and molded lenses. Depending on the geometry, measurements include:

  • Sag and form
  • Front-to-back vertex offset
  • Center thickness and total-thickness variation
  • Wedge and local feature depth
  • Tray-based inspection of multiple optical elements
02

Thin-Film & Optical Characterization

Optical coatings need precise control of film thickness and optical properties. Our thin-film systems use reflectometry, spectrophotometry and ellipsometry to measure coating thickness, refractive index, extinction coefficient, reflectance and transmittance.

They support anti-reflective coatings, optical filters, mirrors, transparent films and multilayer optical structures.

03

Surface & Mechanical Characterization

Optical performance can also depend on surface roughness, coating durability and mechanical properties. We provide equipment to measure roughness, topography, friction, wear, hardness, adhesion, scratch resistance and coating performance.

These measurements support process development and reliability testing for optical glass, coated components and other precision surfaces.

04

Lens Alignment & Optical Assembly

Accurate alignment is critical for multi-element optical systems. Our precision systems measure and align lens centration, tilt, optical axis, radius of curvature and element position during assembly.

Each optical surface can be measured during assembly. That lets you position lenses quantitatively before bonding or fixing, instead of relying only on mechanical references or final system tests. Applications include singlets, cemented lenses, air-spaced assemblies and complex multi-element systems.

Active alignment also supports production inspection of finished lens stacks. It measures beam deviation, radius of curvature, vertex position and air gaps, and aligns reticles, apertures or detector planes inside an assembly.

05

Optical Coatings & Thin-Film Deposition

We provide vacuum deposition equipment for optical coating research, process development and production. Technologies include sputtering, electron-beam and thermal evaporation and other physical vapor deposition (PVD) processes.

Applications include anti-reflective and high-reflective coatings, optical filters, mirrors, beam-control coatings and other functional thin films across ultraviolet, visible and infrared wavelengths.

Specialized coating approaches also support:

  • Low-loss and high laser-damage-threshold optics
  • Infrared imaging and thermal optics
  • Protective diamond-like carbon (DLC) coatings
  • Precision coatings on lenses and other 3D optical shapes

These serve laser systems, imaging, spectroscopy, sensing and demanding environmental applications.

06

Glass & Optical Component Processing

Precision laser micromachining is a flexible, contactless way to process glass, sapphire and other transparent or brittle materials. We support laser cutting, structuring, ablation and selective removal of thin films on optical substrates.

Laser processes produce complex shapes with minimal mechanical load. They can be integrated with automated handling and inspection for production.

07

From Optical Process Development to Production

For unusual optical components or new manufacturing processes, we evaluate feasibility first. Then we combine custom fixtures, motion, handling, inspection and automation around the chosen process, from lab development to integrated production cells.
