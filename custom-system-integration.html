Custom System Integration

Some applications don't fit a standard tool. We build semi- or fully automated systems around your part and process, combining proven instruments with robotics, motion, machine vision, fixtures and software.

Request supportLearn more
Metrology Equipment SalesCalibration & Service

On this page

What we integrateHow a project runsWhere it is used

What we integrate

  • Metrology and inspection instruments
  • Robots and precision motion systems
  • Machine vision and automated handling
  • Custom fixtures, tooling and safety enclosures
  • Control systems, measurement recipes and pass/fail logic
  • Data collection, reporting and connection to factory databases and quality systems

How a project runs

  1. Feasibility testing. We test whether the measurement or process works on your real parts.
  2. Measurement and process development. We define the method, recipe and acceptance criteria with your engineers.
  3. System design. Mechanical design, motion, handling, safety and software are specified around your line.
  4. Fabrication and integration. Fixtures, tooling and assemblies are built, including through contract manufacturing, then integrated and tested.
  5. Installation. The system is installed and commissioned at your site.
  6. Production support. We stay involved with service, recipe updates and improvements.

Where it is used

Custom integration appears across our industries: automated 3D inspection of aerospace and industrial parts, optical assembly cells, advanced packaging assembly, failure-analysis workflows, display repair and photovoltaic laser processing.
