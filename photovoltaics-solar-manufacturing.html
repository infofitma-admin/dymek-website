Photovoltaics & Solar Cell Manufacturing Equipment

Photovoltaic manufacturing combines wafer processing, thin-film deposition, laser processing, precision inspection and high-throughput automation. Dymek provides equipment and engineering for crystalline-silicon and thin-film solar-cell research and production.

Our technologies support single process steps as well as pilot-line development, materials research and specialized next-generation photovoltaic structures.

Thermal Processing, Diffusion & Deposition

We provide furnace and thermal-process equipment for diffusion, oxidation, LPCVD and other high-temperature steps in photovoltaic and semiconductor processing. Systems support R&D, pilot production and selected high-volume applications.

Plasma and vacuum deposition technologies support passivation, functional films and other materials needed in solar-cell structures.

PVD platforms also support solar-absorber and functional thin-film development, with flexible sputtering or evaporation setups for research and scalable production.

Laser Processing of Solar Cells

Laser processing gives high precision without mechanical contact and is widely used in modern PV manufacturing. We support laser contact opening, cell separation, scribing, structuring and other micromachining for crystalline and thin-film solar technologies.

Thermal laser separation divides full silicon cells into half-cells or other formats while minimizing edge damage and keeping mechanical strength.

Flexible & Thin-Film Photovoltaics

Flexible PV materials may need high-speed roll-to-roll patterning of several functional layers. We support laser scribing and isolation for thin-film technologies on flexible substrates, including custom roll-to-roll setups.

Applications span CIGS, perovskite and other emerging thin-film technologies that need precise, non-contact patterning.

Thin-Film & Optical Metrology

Solar-cell performance depends on the thickness and optical properties of many deposited layers. We provide reflectometry, spectrophotometry, ellipsometry and other optical thin-film measurement for film thickness, refractive index, extinction coefficient, reflectance and transmittance.

These measurements support process development, uniformity mapping and production control for coatings, passivation layers, transparent conductive films and other solar-cell materials.

Electrical characterization complements optical metrology. Four-point-probe measurement gives sheet resistance and related properties on wafers, conductive films and cell structures. Wafer- and cell-level probe systems support electrical and optoelectronic characterization of PV devices, including I-V measurement during research, process development and reliability testing.

Advanced & Multi-Junction Solar Research

Next-generation solar cells increasingly combine dissimilar semiconductors, engineered substrates and complex layer stacks. Wafer bonding and heterogeneous integration support research into tandem and multi-junction devices where growing all materials in one structure is difficult. This includes low-temperature integration of III-V layers with silicon and other substrates.

We work with research institutes and manufacturers to evaluate specialized bonding, deposition, laser-processing and metrology needs for emerging PV architectures.

Nanoparticle deposition also supports exploratory work in light harvesting, photovoltaics, catalysis and related energy-conversion materials where particle composition and size are part of the research question.

From Process Development to Production

For production, laser processing, inspection and metrology can be integrated with automated handling, recipe control, data collection and factory interfaces. We support feasibility trials, custom fixtures and process development for non-standard formats or emerging materials before scale-up.
