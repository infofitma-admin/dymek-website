Wafer Processing & Device Fabrication Equipment

Device fabrication depends on tight control of patterning, deposition, etching, thermal treatment, bonding and cleaning. Dymek provides process equipment and engineering support from wafer-level R&D through pilot and volume manufacturing.

We work across silicon, MEMS, sensors, compound semiconductors, SiC/GaN power devices and optoelectronics.

  1. 01

    Lithography & Resist Processing

    We provide lithography and resist-processing solutions for pattern definition at wafer level. Depending on feature size, substrate and production needs, this can include:

    • Resist coating and developing
    • Mask alignment and maskless exposure
    • Nanoimprint lithography

    These technologies support conventional semiconductor patterning. They also suit MEMS, advanced packaging, photonics and other applications that need flexible pattern transfer or non-standard substrates.

  2. 02

    Wafer Bonding, Layer Transfer & Substrate Engineering

    Wafer bonding makes it possible to manufacture MEMS, engineered substrates, image sensors, power devices and compound semiconductor structures. These devices rely on permanent or temporary joining of wafers and layers.

    Bonding capabilities include fusion/direct and plasma-activated bonding, temporary bonding and debonding, thin-wafer handling, layer transfer and aligned bonding. Low-temperature bonding supports heterogeneous integration of dissimilar semiconductor materials and engineered substrates.

    For MEMS and sensors, aligned bonding can also create hermetic or high-vacuum cavity encapsulation. This is used for inertial and environmental sensors, microbolometers, microphones, ultrasonic devices and related structures.

  3. 03

    Plasma Etch, Deposition & Surface Processing

    Plasma processing forms device structures, opens features, deposits functional films and modifies surfaces. We provide equipment for dry etching, plasma-enhanced deposition and surface treatment on silicon, dielectric, compound semiconductor and other materials.

    Applications include MEMS structures, power devices, sensors, photonic devices, microLEDs and other devices that need controlled anisotropic etching or deposited films.

    Vacuum deposition goes beyond conventional metallization. It also covers piezoelectric films for MEMS and BAW/FBAR devices, sensor oxides, contact and barrier layers for GaN and SiC, PIC films and specialized materials for emerging quantum devices.

  4. 04

    Thermal Processing & Furnaces

    Thermal process equipment for oxidation, diffusion, annealing, LPCVD and other high-temperature wafer processes. Configurations suit university and R&D laboratories as well as production environments.

    Thermal processing is used in silicon device fabrication, MEMS, power semiconductors, photovoltaic devices and specialized material systems. Refurbishment and upgrade support may also be available for selected legacy furnaces.

  5. 05

    Wafer Separation, Dicing & Laser Processing

    We support wafer separation and dicing for silicon, SiC and other substrates. The process is chosen by material, device structure, edge quality, particle control, mechanical strength and throughput. Precision laser processing adds local annealing, trimming, scribing and structuring.

    For power semiconductors, selective laser annealing can form ohmic contacts on SiC while limiting heat impact on structures already built. Similar approaches serve magnetic sensors and other specialty devices.

  6. 06

    CMP Process Development & Consumables

    Chemical mechanical polishing (CMP) needs coordinated control of slurry chemistry, pad condition, downforce, speed and conditioning. We support CMP process development and consumables evaluation for semiconductor, MEMS, compound semiconductor, optical and advanced-material substrates.

    Laboratory-scale systems let you:

    • Compare slurries, pads and conditioners
    • Optimize removal rate and uniformity
    • Correlate friction or process conditions with changes in pad and wafer surfaces before recipes move to production
  7. 07

    Process Control & Thin-Film Metrology

    Process development and production need reliable measurement of deposited and patterned layers. Optical metrology measures film thickness, refractive index, extinction coefficient, reflectance, transmittance and multilayer structures for dielectrics, resists, semiconductor layers and coatings.

    Non-contact 3D and thickness metrology adds process control for patterned photoresist, transparent functional layers and microfabricated features. This includes post-development and pre-etch checks on silicon, GaAs, MEMS, LED and VCSEL structures.

    Wafer-level optical metrology also measures flatness, thickness variation and surface finish on polished or patterned substrates. Use it for incoming qualification, polishing control and process monitoring. Non-standard devices can be evaluated through feasibility trials and custom process integration.
