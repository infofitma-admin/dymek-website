Industry · Research

Research & Academia Equipment

Universities, national laboratories and research institutes routinely cross industry lines. Dymek provides research equipment and engineering support for shared facilities, cleanrooms, core laboratories and individual groups working across semiconductors, photonics, materials, energy, life sciences and emerging fields.

No. 01

Electron Microscopy & Advanced Imaging

Scanning and transmission electron microscopy support high-resolution imaging, elemental and structural analysis, biological ultrastructure, tomography, failure analysis and other workflows. Samples span semiconductors, metals, ceramics, polymers, batteries and biological materials.

No. 02

Micro- & Nanofabrication

We support cleanroom and lab processes including lithography, wafer bonding, nanoimprint, plasma etching, deposition, thermal processing, CMP process development and laser micromachining. Equipment is available for research and process development across semiconductors, MEMS, photonics, microfluidics and other microfabricated devices.

Flexible R&D platforms let researchers explore new materials, substrates and process flows before moving to pilot or production scale.

No. 03

Optics & Photonics Research

We provide interferometric optical metrology, thin-film characterization, precision alignment, optical coating equipment, photonic device processing and wafer-level optics technologies.

Applications include precision optics, lasers, waveguides, AR/VR structures, integrated photonics, optical sensors and other experimental optical systems.

Groups building optical instruments can use active alignment to assemble microscope objectives, laser collimators, telescope-related optics and other multi-element systems. Dynamic interferometry supports adaptive optics, MEMS and measurement in vibration-prone or environmental test setups.

No. 04

Quantum & Emerging Device Research

Emerging devices often need materials and process flows that are hard to reproduce with conventional semiconductor methods. We support low-temperature wafer bonding and heterogeneous integration for photonic and quantum-device research, combining silicon, lithium niobate, III-V materials and other specialized platforms.

Thin-film deposition platforms add superconducting films, photonic and sensor layers, piezoelectric materials and wide-bandgap metallization. This gives flexible R&D capability for quantum, RF, MEMS, power-electronics and photonic-device research.

For advanced-materials research, characterization can run under controlled environments, including vacuum, cryogenic or high temperature, humidity and hydrogen exposure. Use it to study friction, wear, coatings and interfaces under realistic conditions.

No. 05

2D Materials, Nanoparticles & Emerging Materials

We support thin-film deposition, low-damage plasma processing and other tools for graphene, 2D materials and emerging functional materials. Controlled low-power processing helps where normal plasma conditions would damage delicate layers or contaminate devices.

Vacuum nanoparticle deposition supports research in:

  • Nanomedicine and biosensors
  • Photovoltaics and light harvesting
  • Fuel-cell catalysis and electrochemistry
  • Environmental purification and surface-enhanced spectroscopies

Systems offer controlled particle size, composition and co-deposition for exploratory materials research.

No. 06

Materials & Surface Characterization

Research labs often need several methods to understand materials and surfaces. We support thin-film thickness and optical-property measurement, tribology, roughness and topography, hardness, adhesion, magnetic characterization, electrical measurement and other analytical techniques.

These tools serve materials science, coatings, energy research, electronics, biomaterials and many other disciplines.

No. 07

Life Sciences & Microfluidics

For biological and diagnostic research, we support electron microscopy, microfluidic fabrication, nanoimprint, precision glass processing and automated inspection. Applications include cell and tissue imaging, biochips, lab-on-chip systems, diagnostic substrates and emerging internal-channel structures in glass.

No. 08

Failure Analysis & Sample Preparation

Research and shared facilities may also need tools to investigate failed devices or prepare difficult samples. We support X-ray, acoustic inspection, automated optical inspection, electron microscopy and laser-based sample preparation for SEM, TEM, X-ray microscopy, atom-probe tomography and micromechanical testing. Laser preparation produces targeted cross-sections, site-specific specimens and custom sample geometries.

No. 09

Advanced Electrical & Device Probing

Flexible probe systems support device research from ultra-low-current and capacitance measurement through high-current, high-voltage power devices, RF and sub-THz characterization, optoelectronic testing and magnetic-field-dependent measurement. Temperature-controlled setups extend testing from cryogenic conditions to several hundred degrees Celsius, depending on the application.

No. 10

Custom Research Systems & Experimental Applications

Novel research doesn't always fit standard equipment. We help evaluate new applications and develop custom fixtures, sample holders, motion, handling, machine vision, integrated experimental systems and data-processing or reporting software.

This flexibility supports exploratory work and gives you a path to repeatable lab, pilot or future production workflows as your application matures.
