Thin-film thickness calculator

Light reflected from the top and bottom of a film interferes and creates fringes. Use them to preview a spectrum or to estimate how thick your film is.

How it works

The maths behind the numbers

1

Interference

Reflections from the film surface and the substrate add or cancel depending on the wavelength, the index n and the thickness d.

2

From fringes to thickness

Two neighbouring peaks at λ₁ and λ₂ give d = m·λ₁·λ₂ / 2n(λ₂−λ₁), where m is the number of fringes between them.

3

Where it stops

Very thin films, absorbing materials and multilayer stacks need a full model fit, as done by spectral reflectometry or ellipsometry tools.
