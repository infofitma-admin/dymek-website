Industry · Displays

Displays & Advanced Optoelectronics Equipment

This sector covers panel- and module-scale optoelectronics: LCD, OLED, microLED/miniLED, smart glass, image sensors and related display or sensing products. Dymek provides process, metrology, assembly, repair, test and automation technologies from device fabrication through panel and module production.

MicroLED, MiniLED & Advanced Emitters

We support laser lift-off, transfer and local repair or rework of microLED, miniLED and related emitters. Precision positioning and automated handling let you transfer or repair individual dies or selected regions without replacing a whole panel or substrate.

Plasma processing, thin-film deposition and wafer-level optoelectronic testing support upstream LED and microLED device fabrication. High-precision die bonding supports assembly of emitters and other optoelectronic components into modules.

Large-Area Patterning & Panel Processes

Large-area nanoimprint lithography replicates micro- and nanoscale structures across panel-size substrates for displays, wire-grid polarizers and other optical functions. The same process family supports structured surfaces in photonic and advanced display applications where optical lithography becomes inefficient over large areas.

Laser processing and custom automation also support selective structuring, trimming and other panel-level processes that need precise, non-contact modification.

Panel, Film & Microstructure Metrology

Display manufacturing needs measurement of transparent films, color-filter structures, photo spacers, cell gaps, retardation, local topography and other features that directly affect optical uniformity. We provide spectroscopic, interferometric and non-contact 3D metrology for these parameters, on research samples and production-scale panels.

Measurements cover color-filter spectral properties, multilayer film thickness, refractive index, surface roughness, panel-glass features, microlens arrays and MEMS or micromirror structures. Small-spot and automated mapping systems help where measurement must target individual patterned regions instead of averaging over a large area.

Optoelectronic Assembly & Sensor Packaging

Advanced displays and related modules can integrate emitters, detectors, image sensors, MEMS, laser sources and optics. We support high-precision die attach, flip-chip/eutectic assembly and related packaging for AR/VR modules, infrared devices, commercial lasers, CMOS image sensors and MEMS products.

Where optical coupling is needed, precision motion and alignment can be integrated with bonding, dispensing, curing and inspection for repeatable production. Active lens alignment also supports AR/VR optical modules, compact imaging assemblies and other sensor products that need accurate centering of multiple elements or alignment of apertures, reticles and detector planes.

Smart Glass, Functional Coatings & Optical Surfaces

We support PVD and other thin-film coating processes for display glass and smart surfaces, including:

  • Hard anti-reflective coatings
  • Transparent conductive oxides
  • Electrochromic layers and controlled transmission or color
  • Anti-fingerprint and other functional optical coatings

Large-area deposition platforms handle sheet and panel substrates. Precision optical coating processes apply to waveguides, sensor optics and other high-performance optical parts used alongside advanced displays.

Electrical, Optical & Reliability Test

Probe systems support flat-panel displays and optoelectronic devices with electrical, optical and temperature-dependent measurements. Applications include sheet resistance, I-V and C-V characterization, RF or high-speed measurement and combined optical/electrical testing of LEDs, VCSELs, photodiodes and related devices.

Inspection, X-ray, microscopy and other analytical methods add failure analysis when defects start in packaging, interconnects or device structures rather than the visible panel surface.

From Process Development to Production

New display architectures often combine processes that no single standard tool offers. We integrate motion, machine vision, handling, metrology, repair, process equipment and software around your application, from R&D feasibility through pilot and volume automation.
