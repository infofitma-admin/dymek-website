Advanced Packaging & Assembly Equipment

Advanced packaging is now a critical part of semiconductor performance. It brings multiple dies, materials and functions together in ever more compact systems. Dymek provides process and assembly technologies for wafer-level packaging, heterogeneous integration, 2.5D/3D integration and next-generation device architectures.

Our solutions support process development through high-volume manufacturing for chiplets, high-bandwidth memory (HBM), fan-out packages, system-in-package (SiP), optical modules and other advanced assemblies.

Layer01

Wafer-to-Wafer & Die-to-Wafer Bonding

We support fusion, direct and hybrid bonding for wafer-to-wafer and die-to-wafer integration. These technologies create fine-pitch electrical and mechanical connections between device layers. They are increasingly important for 3D integration, image sensors, logic-memory integration, chiplets and HBM.

A complete process flow can include surface preparation, cleaning, activation, alignment, bonding and post-bond metrology.

Layer02

Temporary Bonding, Debonding & Thin-Wafer Handling

Thin wafers need reliable mechanical support while they are thinned, processed and transferred. We provide temporary bonding and debonding, carrier handling and layer-release processes for advanced packaging, power devices and other thin-substrate applications.

These processes support backside processing, redistribution layers (RDL), fan-out packaging and other workflows where wafer fragility and warpage must be carefully controlled.

Layer03

Precision Die Placement & Thermocompression

Advanced packages need high-accuracy placement of known-good die onto wafers, interposers or substrates. We support precision die bonding, flip-chip assembly, thermocompression bonding and related placement processes for fine-pitch interconnects and heterogeneous devices.

Applications range from R&D and low-volume assembly to production systems for multi-die packages and high-density integration.

Layer04

2.5D, 3D, Chiplets & HBM

High-performance computing and AI are speeding up the adoption of chiplet architectures, silicon interposers and vertically stacked memory. We support the bonding, alignment, thin-wafer processing and inspection steps these structures require.

The same capabilities apply to other high-density packages that combine logic, memory, sensors, RF, photonics or specialty devices in one package.

Layer05

Optical & Photonic Packaging

As optical and electronic functions converge, packaging needs precise alignment between photonic devices, lenses, fiber arrays and electronic components. We support optical component placement, fiber-array alignment, lens alignment and related assembly for optical modules, co-packaged optics and optical I/O.

These applications combine semiconductor packaging methods with active or passive optical alignment and high-precision motion.

High-precision die placement and optical coupling also support specialty optoelectronic and sensor packages, such as:

  • LiDAR emitters and receivers
  • Commercial laser modules and infrared devices
  • MEMS and CMOS image sensors
  • RF and hybrid devices

Depending on the application, assembly can combine eutectic, sintering, thermocompression, adhesive or other bonding methods with automated alignment and handling.

Layer06

Package Inspection & Process Control

Advanced packages contain buried interfaces that visual inspection cannot always reach. We provide X-ray and CT inspection, scanning acoustic microscopy, automated optical inspection and other metrology techniques. They detect voids, delamination, die cracks, underfill defects, Cu-pillar issues, solder defects and other package abnormalities.

Inspection data supports process development, incoming quality control, production monitoring and failure analysis. For targeted root-cause work, laser-based sample preparation can quickly produce large-area package cross-sections. It can also expose regions of interest in interposers, TSV/RDL structures, stacked dies and hybrid-bonded interfaces before microscopy or ion-beam analysis.

Non-contact 3D surface metrology adds measurement of package and substrate topography, warpage, transparent-layer thickness and fine feature geometry. This helps process development for advanced substrates, redistribution structures and other package elements where dimensional variation affects alignment or bonding.

Layer07

From Assembly Development to Production

For specialized packages, we combine custom fixtures, precision motion, handling, machine vision and process software around the core equipment. Projects can move from feasibility trials and process integration into semi- or fully automated production.
