Industry · Electronics

Electronics Assembly, Test & Reliability

This sector covers what happens after semiconductor devices leave the fab: assembly into PCBs, modules and finished systems, followed by inspection, EMC development, reliability testing and failure analysis. Dymek supports SMT and PCB manufacturing, high-density interconnects, electronic modules and other finished hardware.

TP01

SMT, PCB & Module Inspection

We provide inspection technologies for solder joints, component placement, connectors, packages and other features on PCBs and electronic modules. Automated optical inspection and machine vision find visible defects and process variation. X-ray inspection reaches hidden interconnects and internal structures.

Non-contact 3D metrology adds dimensional control of PCB substrates and fine features:

  • Line width and spacing
  • Board or substrate warpage
  • Via size, depth and position

This is useful during process development and incoming quality control, before assembly.

TP02

PCB & High-Density Interconnect Processing

As assemblies move to finer pitch and higher density, smaller vias and thinner multilayer structures get harder to process consistently. We support precision laser drilling and ablation for microvias and other fine PCB or interconnect features, where feature size, geometry or material mix challenge conventional methods.

For emerging applications, process development can add real-time monitoring and endpoint control. This improves repeatability and lowers the risk of drilling through or damaging underlying layers.

TP03

X-Ray & CT for Electronics

2D X-ray, 3D CT and automated inline X-ray reveal defects you can't see from the surface. These include solder voids, bridging, insufficient solder, hidden joints, package defects and internal assembly problems.

These systems support process development, production quality control, incoming inspection and lab failure analysis across SMT, power electronics, automotive electronics and other assemblies.

TP04

EMC Development & Pre-Compliance

Electromagnetic compatibility problems can start at IC, PCB or system level. We provide tools for emissions and immunity investigation, near-field scanning, ESD-related analysis and IC-level EMC testing.

They help engineers locate interference sources, understand coupling paths and improve designs before formal compliance testing.

TP05

RF, Wireless & High-Frequency Electronics

RF and wireless electronics need specialized materials, packaging and test methods at frequencies where conventional electrical approaches get difficult. We support thin-film deposition for piezoelectric MEMS, BAW/FBAR and other RF devices, plus high-precision assembly of RF, microwave and hybrid-device packages.

On-wafer probing supports RF and sub-THz characterization alongside DC, pulsed and temperature-dependent measurements. Developers can evaluate high-frequency performance before final assembly.

TP06

Package & Assembly Failure Analysis

When failures start inside packages or assemblies, non-destructive acoustic inspection, X-ray and high-resolution microscopy help identify delamination, voids, cracks, die-attach issues, solder defects and other hidden problems.

Electron microscopy adds structural and materials detail for root-cause analysis. Laser-based sample preparation removes bulk material and exposes targeted regions in packages or system-level boards before microscopy or ion-beam analysis. Typical targets are large-area package cross-sections, flip-chip assemblies and solder joints or balls that are hard to prepare mechanically without deformation or artefacts.

TP07

Production Data & Reporting

Inspection and test equipment generates large volumes of quality data across boards, modules and finished systems. We support automated collection, correlation and reporting of inspection and test results to improve traceability and reduce manual engineering work.

Custom scripts and software integrations process instrument data, generate reports and connect results to production databases, quality systems or other manufacturing software.

TP08

Materials, Coatings & Reliability

Electronic products may also need characterization of coatings, adhesives, contact surfaces and mechanical interfaces. We support surface roughness, friction, wear, hardness, adhesion and other materials measurements during product development and reliability evaluation.

Electrical-connector testing can link fretting wear and corrosion to real-time current, voltage and contact behavior under controlled environments. For specialized production, inspection can also be integrated with handling, machine vision and factory software.
