Automotive & E-Mobility Manufacturing and Inspection

Automotive manufacturing now combines mechanical systems with batteries, power electronics, sensors and advanced electronic assemblies. Dymek provides inspection, materials-characterization, semiconductor, optical and automation technologies, from component development and incoming inspection through production quality control and reliability testing.

Vehicle & Component Inspection

Automated, non-contact inspection combines optical 3D measurement, machine vision and robotics. It handles dimensional control and defect detection on trim, castings, machined or molded parts and other vehicle components.

Portable or automated surface metrology measures paint and coating texture, scratches, pits, edge features and roughness directly on parts or body surfaces. This links surface preparation with final appearance and component quality.

02

Materials, Coatings & Tribology

Friction, wear, coatings and surface condition directly affect efficiency, noise, durability and reliability. We provide tribology and surface-characterization equipment for bearings, gears, brakes, lubricants, coatings, electrical contacts and e-drive systems.

Measurements include friction, wear, fretting, adhesion, hardness, roughness, film thickness and coating durability under controlled temperature, load and environment. Typical uses:

  • Brake-material screening
  • Lubrication and wear in electrified drivetrains
  • Connector fretting with live electrical-contact monitoring
  • Scratch and mar resistance of paints and exterior coatings
03

EV Batteries & Electronic Assemblies

Electric vehicles add inspection needs for batteries, power modules and high-density electronic assemblies. We support 2D X-ray, 3D CT and automated X-ray inspection for battery structures, solder joints, die-cast components and electronic modules.

Optical or automated inspection can be added where surface or dimensional quality must be checked alongside internal features.

04

Power Semiconductors

SiC and GaN devices are increasingly important in traction inverters, charging systems and other high-voltage vehicle electronics. We provide wafer processing, laser annealing, inspection and failure-analysis technologies for developing and manufacturing wide-bandgap power devices.

Capabilities include thermal and plasma processing, selective laser annealing, laser trimming, wafer separation and dicing, thin-film metrology, wafer inspection and package analysis.

Wafer-level electrical characterization supports high-current, high-voltage testing of SiC and GaN power devices across controlled temperature ranges. This helps evaluate performance and reliability before packaging.

05

Sensors & MEMS

Vehicles rely on MEMS and magnetic sensors for motion, pressure, steering, throttle, safety and driver-assistance functions. We support wafer bonding, microfabrication, magnetic-sensor processing, inspection and characterization for these devices.

For MEMS that need controlled cavity conditions, aligned wafer bonding provides hermetic or high-vacuum encapsulation for inertial sensors, microbolometers and other sensing devices used in automotive and autonomous systems.

Precision optical and photonic technologies also support sensing and imaging applications that need optical components or alignment.

06

LiDAR, Cameras & Optical Sensors

Driver-assistance and autonomous systems depend on LiDAR, cameras, infrared imaging and other optical sensors. We support precision die attach and optoelectronic packaging for emitters, receivers, CMOS image sensors and IR modules. We also support active alignment of lenses, camera objectives, detector planes, apertures and reticles where optical-axis accuracy affects performance.

Specialized coatings support:

  • High-performance LiDAR optics
  • Driver-monitoring and front/rear camera optics
  • Night-vision systems, rain and ambient-light sensors
  • Protective sensor covers and smart or electrochromic vehicle glass
07

EMC, Reliability & Failure Analysis

As vehicles carry more high-speed electronics and power conversion, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and reliability matter more. We support PCB- and IC-level EMC development, X-ray and acoustic inspection, electron microscopy and other failure-analysis techniques for automotive electronics. High-resolution analysis also supports investigation of power-semiconductor metallization and interconnects after electrical and thermal stress testing.

08

From Feasibility to Production

We integrate robotics, machine vision, metrology, handling, fixtures and production software around non-standard components or processes. Optional additions include optimization, dashboards, reporting and inspection/test-data integration.
