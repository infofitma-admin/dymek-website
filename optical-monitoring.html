Where the real limit is

Your vacuum coater may still be capable of producing the films you need.

The limitation may instead be how accurately the growing multilayer structure is monitored and controlled.

As optical designs become more complex, small thickness errors can accumulate across many layers and lead to spectral shift, reduced transmission, inadequate blocking or poor run-to-run repeatability.

The i-Photonics OCP Optical Monitoring System is designed to add direct optical process control to coating systems using technologies including evaporation, ion-assisted deposition, magnetron sputtering, IBS and PARMS.

Why optical monitoring becomes critical

For complex coatings, the question is no longer “Did we deposit the intended thickness?” It becomes “Is the developing optical spectrum still on target?”

A recent i-Photonics narrowband-filter example illustrates the challenge: approximately 170 dielectric layers, around 20 µm total coating thickness and a continuous deposition cycle of approximately 48 hours. i-Photonics notes that optical monitoring was maintained throughout the multilayer deposition.
