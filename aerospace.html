Aerospace Manufacturing & MRO Inspection Equipment

Aerospace manufacturing and maintenance need accurate, repeatable inspection of critical components, surfaces and materials. Dymek provides metrology, inspection and materials-characterization equipment for new-part manufacturing and for maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO).

Our technologies support defect inspection, dimensional measurement, surface characterization, materials testing, failure analysis and automated quality control. We can also build inspection equipment into custom systems designed around your part and process.

Discuss your applicationSee capabilities

Portable & In-Situ Defect Inspection

Portable 3D inspection systems let engineers measure defects directly on large or hard-to-move parts. There is no need to send them to a metrology lab. Non-contact systems measure scratches, pits, nicks, dents, corrosion, edge breaks, radii and other surface features.

Typical applications include turbine blades, engine components, machined parts and hard-to-reach surfaces. Quantitative 3D measurement reduces reliance on visual inspection and manual gauges. Engineers make faster, more consistent repair, rework and acceptance decisions.

Automated 3D Inspection & Dimensional Metrology

We support automated 3D inspection of aerospace components using non-contact optical measurement, machine vision, robotics and precision motion. These systems check dimensional features, surface geometry and production defects right on the factory floor.

Applications include complex machined components, composite structures, castings and large assemblies where coordinate measurement or manual inspection is slow or hard to automate.

Materials, Coatings & Tribology

We provide laboratory equipment for testing aerospace materials, coatings and lubricants. It measures friction, wear, fretting, adhesion, hardness, surface roughness and coating performance.

Tests can run under controlled conditions such as high or cryogenic temperature, vacuum, humidity and high mechanical loads. This supports development and qualification of engine components, bearings, gears, actuators, composites, lubricants and aerospace coatings.

Microscopy & Failure Analysis

When defects need closer investigation, electron microscopy and related techniques support materials analysis, fracture investigation, contamination analysis and root-cause studies.

These tools complement dimensional and surface inspection with detail on microstructure, interfaces and failure mechanisms.

Aerospace & Defense Optical Assembly

Aerospace, space and defense systems can contain large-aperture or deep multi-element optical assemblies. Mechanical references alone often cannot reach the required optical-axis accuracy. We support non-contact active alignment of lenses and optical surfaces during assembly, including large imaging systems, laser collimators, telescope-related assemblies and other precision optical modules.

For infrared and electro-optical systems, we also support specialized thin-film coatings for thermal imaging, night vision and other IR optics, including protective and low-loss coatings for harsh environments.

Automated Inspection & Custom Metrology Systems

We develop semi- or fully automated inspection systems around your component and production process. We integrate metrology equipment with robots, precision motion, machine vision, part handling and production software.

Custom fixtures and part-handling systems position components accurately and repeatably during inspection. We can also deliver:

  • Mechanical design and safety enclosures
  • Control systems and measurement recipes
  • Automated data collection and pass/fail reporting
  • Connection to factory databases and existing quality systems

From Feasibility to Production

When your application needs custom hardware, we support the design and contract manufacturing of fixtures, tooling, mechanical assemblies and other system components. We manage projects from feasibility testing and measurement development through system design, fabrication, integration, installation and production support.
