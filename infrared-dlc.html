A distinctive coating challenge

Infrared research asks more of a coating than transmission alone.

Infrared research creates a distinctive coating challenge.

Researchers may need high transmission across MWIR or LWIR wavelengths while also addressing adhesion, environmental durability, abrasion resistance and the particular characteristics of substrates such as germanium, silicon, ZnS and chalcogenide glasses.

i-Photonics develops PIAD, PARMS and Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC) coating technologies for infrared optical applications, and Dymek supports them across Southeast Asia.

Target IR applications

Infrared research areas

i-Photonics develops PIAD, PARMS and diamond-like carbon (DLC) coating technologies engineered specifically for demanding IR environments.

DIAMANTA PECVD platform

DLC for durable infrared optics

The Diamanta platform uses PECVD technology to deposit diamond-like carbon coatings for IR optics, particularly for external optical components where infrared performance must be combined with mechanical and environmental durability: thermal cameras, optical windows and other IR systems.

  • High hardness
  • Abrasion resistance
  • Rain & sand erosion protection

Target substrates:
Germanium (Ge): LWIR windows & thermal lenses
Silicon (Si): lightweight MWIR optical components
Zinc sulfide (ZnS): multispectral & LWIR windows
Chalcogenides: moulded IR glass substrates
